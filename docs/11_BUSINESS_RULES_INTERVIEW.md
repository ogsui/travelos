# 11 Business Rules Interview (owner + accountant)

No coding agent can invent these correctly. Spend an hour with the owner and a second with the accountant. Capture answers in `config/business_rules.yaml` (example provided). Bring 20 real past quotes (names removed) and 10 real invoices.

## A. Quoting & margins (owner)
1. How do you set margin per product: flights (domestic/international), hotels, packages, transfers, visas, insurance? Fixed fee, percentage, tiered?
2. Minimum acceptable margin per product? When do you quote below it?
3. Discounts: who gets them, how much, who approves? Corporate/repeat/referral/group rules?
4. How long are quotes valid? What happens if the fare changes after the customer says yes?
5. Show customers 1 option or 3? How do you structure family-holiday quotes (day-wise itinerary, inclusions/exclusions)?
6. Standard terms & conditions, cancellation and refund policy, amendment fees.
7. Payment terms: advance %, instalment schedule, deadlines by product. Accepted payment methods.
8. How do you currently compare against MakeMyTrip etc.? What undercut/premium is acceptable?
9. Seasonal calendar: peak/off-peak by destination; festival/holiday pushes.

## B. Operations (owner/staff)
10. Walk through a typical day: inbound channels, response times, who handles what.
11. Which tasks eat the most time? (Time one real quote end to end.)
12. Supplier mix: which portals for which products; wallet/credit mechanics; who funds suppliers and when.
13. Rules for ticketing time limits, holds, group blocks.
14. Disruption handling: who is on call, what do you offer customers?
15. Common objections and how you answer them. Top 10 FAQs.
16. Visa services: which countries, which sources do you trust, what do you charge?
17. Insurance: do you sell it? Under what licence/partner?

## C. Customers & consent (owner)
18. Customer list: how many, what fields, where stored (Excel?), how were contacts collected, any opt-in evidence?
19. Which customers should never be messaged? Corporate contacts with special terms?
20. Referral/reward ideas you already use.
21. Brand voice: samples of messages/posts you like; languages used (English/Hindi/Hinglish/regional).

## D. Accounting & tax (accountant)
22. GST treatment per product type (commission vs principal/margin basis) and invoice formats currently used.
23. TCS on overseas packages: applicability, thresholds, process.
24. Invoice series, numbering, financial-year reset, credit-note practice.
25. Master Excel/tax workbook structure — share the actual files; Tally/Zoho usage.
26. Payment gateway fees: who bears them? Reconciliation process.
27. Monthly accountant pack: what do you need and in what format?
28. Record retention periods for invoices, passport copies, messages.

## E. Marketing (owner)
29. Existing social accounts, who owns logins, content that performed well.
30. Monthly ad budget caps (Google/Meta) and target CAC.
31. Destinations/products to push; seasonal offers; margins by product to prioritise.
32. Photo/video library and licences.
33. Competitors you watch and which of their prices matter.

## F. Governance (owner)
34. Who approves what? Value thresholds for four-eyes? Who covers when you're unavailable?
35. Which actions are you comfortable automating first (see `05_APPROVAL_MATRIX.md`)? What would make you switch the system off?
36. Staff roles and phone numbers for urgent paging.

## Output
Fill `config/business_rules.yaml`; list unresolved questions in `docs/DECISIONS.md`; get the owner and accountant to initial the final file. Tasks 015, 011, 021 and 026 are not "done" until the relevant sections are filled.
